# Challenge 4 - Make it look like a real store

**[Home](../Readme.md)** - [Previous Challenge](challenge-03.md) - [Next Challenge](finish.md)

## Goal

Turn the working chat into the **Summit & Spoke** outdoor and bike store website, and give it a winter vibe.

## Actions

* Restyle the site so it looks like a real outdoor and bike store (prompt 11).
* Give the store a winter look (prompt 12).

## Success criteria

* The site looks like a store, with the AI gear expert still working.
* Chat, product search and `CosmosDBadmin.html` still work as before.
* The site stays usable on a 400 px-wide screen and with the keyboard only.
* User, model and product text is still rendered as text, not as HTML.
